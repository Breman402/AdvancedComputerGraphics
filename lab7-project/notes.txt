There are 2 vertex arrays, the sun and the globe.

The function create sphere mesh:
    has a custom datatype, Vertex, which in turn has a position, normal and texcoord and is used INTERNALLY.
    
    It loops through the latitude and longitude, calculating what each vertex's position, normal and texcoord shlould be.

    It proceeds to then build the index buffer, turning the grid of sphere vertices into triangles, every 6 indices define
    2 triangles.

    The vertex position is then sent to attrib0, the normal attrib1 and the texcoord attrib2.

The load texture function:
    had to disable stbi_set_flip_vertically_on_load

    Generates a mipmap from the texture

    Does filtering, can do useGL_NEAREST, does AnisotropicFiltering

    wrapps the texture

The shadow pass in display:
    1. Render into shadowmap instead of the screen
    2. bind the height map so that the light gets to see the geometry that the camera sees.
    3. draw the mesh into the depth texture
